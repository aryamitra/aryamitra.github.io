How it works

  1. 1Upload or paste your resume. Files are read in your browser.
  2. 2Your text is embedded and compared against UMass student job postings that are still listed and open.
  3. 3Browse ranked matches and see why each one fits. Mark bad fits to improve the ranking, then apply or email the contact directly.

Privacy

  • Nothing is saved on our end. There are no accounts and no database of users. Your resume text never leaves your browser: it is turned into a list of numbers on your device, and only those numbers are sent to look up matching jobs.
  • Your filters, saved jobs, application tracker and "not a fit" feedback are saved only on your device, by this site (in the browser's site storage). Nobody else can see them, and they are never sold or shared.
  • Job data comes from UMass's public Student Employment board.
